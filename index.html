The Day Everything Changed: A Director's Journey

In May 2025, I faced a "board meeting" that no amount of corporate experience could prepare me for. My 87-year-old mother slipped in our kitchen. From that moment, my life shifted from the boardroom to the sofa next to her bedroom.

I realized that caregiving is a world of silent logistics. We struggled with dignity, hygiene, and the sudden need for professional tools. I founded VIIcare because I lived through 12 months of uncertainty. I know the fear of the first fall—and I am here to help you navigate it.
